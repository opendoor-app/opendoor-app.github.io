You were free at 2pm Tuesday. So was your best friend. Neither of you knew.

  • 02
    Plans take a week.

    Five calendars, three "what about Thursday?"s, one cancellation. The hangout happens in two weeks, if at all.

How it works

Three taps from "free" to "found you."

  1. 1

    Open your door

    Tap once to say you're free. Add how long (15 minutes or two hours) and, if you want, a vibe and a note like "library, 2nd floor."

  2. 2 BestiesRoommatesFamilyStudy Group

    Choose your circle

    Pick who gets the knock: a whole circle, a few circles, or just two specific people. Nobody else sees it.

  3. 3 MI'm coming

    Friends drop in

    Whoever's around taps "I'm coming" and heads your way. When your time's up, your door closes on its own.

Features

Everything you need to catch your people. Nothing you don't.

One tap says "I'm free"

Open your door for 15 minutes, 90, or whatever you've got. Add a vibe (coffee, study, food, walk) or write your own. It closes by itself, so there's nothing to cancel and no awkward "never mind."

Errands, with company

"Heading to Target in 20." Friends tap I'm in or Grab me one. That's the whole conversation.

You pick the guest list

Besties, Family, Roommates, Study Group, or just Maya and Jordan. Every door gets its own guest list.

Replies, not a group chat

One tap to say you're coming, one quiet knock to let them know. No thread, no roll call, no 47 unread.

Words, never GPS

Say "at the library" if you want. We never track where you are, and every door expires.

Find the overlap

Add your class or work schedule once, or connect your calendar. Open Door spots when you and a friend are both free.

Close, even when you're far

Mom's two time zones away? See when she's free and what time it is there, so the call actually lands.

Show up a little more

Private streaks and badges for the days you open your door. No leaderboard, no likes, no follower counts. Just a gentle nudge toward the people who matter.

Privacy & trust

Built so you can relax.

Sharing your free time should feel like leaving your door open, not like being watched. Here's what we promise never to do.

Read our privacy promise →
  • No location tracking

    We never ask for GPS, not even in the background. If you want to say where you are, you type it.

  • No public feed

    Nothing is posted to the world. Only the circles or people you pick ever see your door.

  • No posts that linger

    Doors close on their own and plans expire, so last Tuesday isn't still floating around.

  • No "seen" pressure

    Nobody knows who saw their door and didn't come. Showing up is always optional.

FAQ

Good questions.

Something else on your mind? Email us at iab101@miami.edu.

Is Open Door free?
Yes. Opening your door, circles and replies are free. If we add optional extras later, the basics stay free.
Who can see when my door is open?
Only the circles or specific people you choose for that door. There's no public profile and no feed for strangers. You can also go invisible, snooze notifications, or mute anyone, and they won't know.
Does it track my location?
No. Open Door never uses GPS. If you want people to know where you are, you add a short note like "at the library," and it disappears when your door closes.
What if I see an open door but can't go?
Nothing happens. Nobody can see who saw their door and didn't come, so there's no guilt and no "seen at 2:14."
Do my friends need the app too?
To see your door, yes. Inviting them takes a few seconds: share your link, or find friends who are already on Open Door from your contacts.
When does it launch?
We have a working prototype now and are testing it with students. Waitlist members get in first, starting with college campuses. You can try the prototype today.
Will it be on iPhone and Android?
That's the plan. We're designing Open Door for both from day one, because your friends don't all have the same phone.
