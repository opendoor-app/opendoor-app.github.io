Privacy

Our privacy promise

Open Door is about sharing a little of your time, never your every move. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect and what we never will.

Where we are today. Open Door is in the prototype stage. The only thing this website collects is the email you give us for the waitlist. A full privacy policy will be published before the app launches.

What we never do

  • No location tracking. We don't ask for GPS access, in the foreground or the background. Any "where" in the app is a short note you type yourself.
  • No public feed. Your door is only shown to the circles or people you choose for it.
  • No selling your data. We don't sell or rent your information to advertisers or anyone else.
  • No "seen" receipts. Nobody can tell who saw their door and didn't respond.

What the app will use, and why

  • Your account: name, school email and username, so friends can find you.
  • Your friends and circles: so we know who should see each door.
  • Doors and plans you post: kept only until they expire, then removed.
  • Your schedule (optional): busy and free times you add, or a calendar you connect, used only to find shared free windows. Friends see free or busy, never event names.
  • Contacts (optional): matched on your device to find friends already on Open Door. We don't upload your address book.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we use your email only to tell you when Open Door is ready for you. Every message will have an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your email at any time.

Your controls

In the app you can go invisible, snooze notifications, mute people or circles, leave a circle, or delete your account. Deleting your account removes your profile, circles and schedule.

Questions

Email us at iab101@miami.edu and a real person on our team will answer.
